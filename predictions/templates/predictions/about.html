About

This site presents model-implied matchup probabilities for games across multiple sports and leagues. The numbers estimate what the model thinks is most likely to happen based on history, team strength, recent form, rest, schedule and availability.

These are not sportsbook odds, betting prices, or recommendations. Treat low-confidence and weak-data rows with extra caution.

How it works

  • Predictions are imported from the analysis pipeline's report files (matchup_predictions_today.csv) via python manage.py import_predictions.
  • The owner approves, edits or hides predictions in the Django admin.
  • The parlay creator lets you combine approved picks into a parlay and see the combined model probability and fair odds.
  • New manual matchups added through Add are drafted by the AI reasoning service and held for review before they appear.
